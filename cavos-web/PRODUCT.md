# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cavos is used by developers and support engineers integrating device-native smart accounts across Starknet, Solana, and Stellar. They need to configure an integration, verify that Cavos-controlled flows work, diagnose failures, and understand sponsorship and recovery without monitoring a user's financial life.

## Product Purpose

The developer console is the operational surface for Cavos. It exposes the state Cavos can verify: wallet registration, device authorization, recovery coordination, relayer decisions, gas sponsorship, configuration health, and delivery status. Success means an integration issue can be identified and acted on in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Precise, sober, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

- A block explorer that reconstructs balances, holdings, NFTs, or unrelated on-chain activity.
- A decorative crypto dashboard dominated by gradients, token prices, or vanity charts.
- A settings maze made of undifferentiated cards and modal dialogs.
- An opaque health score that hides the checks behind it.

## Design Principles

1. Show only what Cavos knows and can explain.
2. Prefer actionable operational state over vanity metrics.
3. Keep production context unmistakable without visual alarmism.
4. Reveal sensitive identifiers deliberately and audit the action.
5. Make the common path quiet; reserve emphasis for states requiring action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic status announcements, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and layouts down to 320px.
