This guide lists [embedded wallet](/embedded-wallet) SDKs that support **Solana**, based on each vendor's own documentation as of October 2026. Cavos, LLC publishes this page and Cavos is on the list, so the criteria come first and every row links to the source. Solana has the deepest bench of any chain here, and several of these providers are strong choices.

For the multichain view, see [embedded wallet providers](/blog/embedded-wallet-providers). For head-to-head pages, see [compare](/compare).

## How we evaluated

- **Solana depth.** Create, sign, and send, or signing only.
- **Fee payment.** Can the app pay fees or let users pay in a token other than SOL?
- **Custody.** Who can produce a signature.
- **Platforms.** Web, React Native, and native mobile.
- **Pricing meter.** Wallets, monthly active users, or signatures.

## When each one fits

- **Cavos** fits consumer Solana apps that want device-held keys, sponsored or token-paid fees, and the same SDK on Stellar and Starknet. It does not cover EVM.
- **Privy** fits teams that want a finished auth plus wallet product with managed sending on Solana and EVM.
- **Dynamic** fits apps that mix embedded and external wallets across many chains.
- **Turnkey** fits teams building their own wallet UX or server-side flows with strict signing policies.
- **Magic**, **MetaMask Embedded Wallets**, **Para**, and **Crossmint** fit teams already on those platforms for other chains or products.

## Where Cavos stands on Solana

Cavos is a non-custodial embedded wallet for Starknet, Solana and Stellar. On Solana it creates a native Ed25519 system account whose key is derived on the device, with Gas Sponsorship in Any Token through a relayer. Read the [Solana wallet page](/embedded-solana-wallet), the [custody model](/custody), [pricing](/pricing), and the [quickstart](https://docs.cavos.xyz/docs/quickstart).
