## The short answer (November 2026)

**Best embedded wallets for Solana in 2026** is a custody and fee-payer question. Most vendors in this table can create and sign a Solana wallet. They do not share a key model.

- **Cavos:** native Ed25519 system account, device-native key, relayer as fee payer or fee settled in a held token such as USDC.
- **Privy:** managed send on Solana/SVM (tier 3). Gas sponsorship available on that tier. TEE user wallets; optional custodial path via Bridge.
- **Dynamic:** JS/React Tier 1 send-and-sign with gasless listed. TSS-MPC. Strong when you also connect Phantom.
- **Turnkey:** SVM at signing, parsing, and policy depth. Custody is who authorizes the enclave.
- **Magic, MetaMask Embedded Wallets, Para, Crossmint:** Solana is documented on each stack. Use them when you are already on that vendor for other chains.

Pick on who can produce a signature without the user, who pays the fee, and what the invoice meters. Published list prices are [how much embedded wallets cost](/blog/embedded-wallet-cost).

## How we evaluated

- **Solana depth.** Create, sign, and send, or signing only.
- **Fee payment.** Can the app pay fees or let users pay in a token other than SOL?
- **Custody.** Who can produce a signature.
- **Platforms.** Web, React Native, and native mobile.
- **Pricing meter.** Wallets, monthly active users, or signatures.

## When each one fits

- **Cavos** fits consumer Solana apps that want device-held keys, sponsored or token-paid fees, and the same SDK on Stellar and Starknet. It does not cover EVM.
- **Privy** fits teams that want a finished auth plus wallet product with managed sending on Solana and EVM.
- **Dynamic** fits apps that mix embedded and external wallets across many chains.
- **Turnkey** fits teams building their own wallet UX or server-side flows with strict signing policies.
- **Magic**, **MetaMask Embedded Wallets**, **Para**, and **Crossmint** fit teams already on those platforms for other chains or products.

Cavos is a non-custodial embedded wallet for Starknet, Solana and Stellar.
