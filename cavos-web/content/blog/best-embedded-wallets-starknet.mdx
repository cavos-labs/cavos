- **Cavos:** audited OpenZeppelin AccountUpgradeable v3.0.0, stark key derived on the device, paymaster sponsorship. Connect never deploys; the first `wallet.execute(calls)` deploys and runs atomically. Free up to 1,000 wallets.

## How we evaluated

- **Starknet depth.** Deploys an account and executes multicalls, or only produces a key.
- **Account contract.** Which implementation and whether it is audited.
- **Gas.** Paymaster sponsorship or user-funded. Gas Sponsorship in Any Token versus you pay ETH or STRK.
- **Custody.** Who can produce a signature.
- **Fit.** General consumer apps, games, or payments.

## Paymaster and sponsored gas

Who pays gas decides whether a new user can transact before they hold ETH or STRK.

Cavos routes `wallet.execute(calls)` through a SNIP-29 paymaster (with SRC-9 outside execution). That is part of Gas Sponsorship in Any Token. In `@cavos/kit` 0.5.2 you pass `paymasterApiKey` into `Cavos.connect` or `CavosProvider`. Treat that key as a client-visible, app-scoped sponsorship credential, never as an operator or treasury secret. Connect never deploys. The first execute deploys AccountUpgradeable and runs the multicall in one sponsored transaction. After deploy, `fee: "self"` pays from the account's own balance. A self-funded first execute is refused, because an undeployed account has no balance to deploy itself with.

Chipi Pay states that its paymaster pays gas for its wallets. Cartridge Controller: see Cartridge docs for sponsorship. Privy requires Tier 3 for gas sponsorship, and Starknet is not on that tier, so you fund gas yourself. MetaMask Embedded Wallets leaves the gas setup to you.

## Where Cavos stands on Starknet

Cavos is a non-custodial embedded wallet for Starknet, Solana and Stellar. On Starknet it deploys an audited OpenZeppelin account contract (AccountUpgradeable v3.0.0) controlled by a stark key derived on the device. Keys never leave the device in plaintext. Signing always happens on the device. Cavos cannot see the key, sign with it, decrypt it, or move funds. A new device restores the MasterDEK with another signed-in device, a passkey, or opt-in hardware-isolated enclave recovery. The passkey and the enclave only hold an encrypted copy of the MasterDEK for sealed restore; they cannot sign, and Cavos cannot decrypt that copy.

The Cavos paymaster sponsors deployment and execution, which is part of Gas Sponsorship in Any Token. [Pricing](/pricing) is free up to 1,000 wallets, then Essential at $59/mo and Complete at $139/mo. Hosted login is Google, Apple, or an enclave-verified email code. Telegram is optional web-only OAuth via oauth.telegram.org in kit 0.5.2; it is not a Telegram Mini App and is not available in React Native.
