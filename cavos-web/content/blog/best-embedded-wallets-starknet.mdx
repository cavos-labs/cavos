This guide lists [embedded wallet](/embedded-wallet) options for **Starknet**, based on each vendor's own documentation as of October 2026. Cavos, LLC publishes this page and Cavos is on the list, so the criteria come first and every row links to the source.

Starknet has native account abstraction: every wallet is an account contract. That makes two questions matter more than on other chains: which account contract the user gets, and who pays the gas. For background, see [gasless Starknet transactions](/blog/gasless-starknet-transactions).

## How we evaluated

- **Starknet depth.** Deploys an account and executes multicalls, or only produces a key.
- **Account contract.** Which implementation and whether it is audited.
- **Gas.** Paymaster sponsorship or user-funded.
- **Custody.** Who can produce a signature.
- **Fit.** General consumer apps, games, or payments.

## When each one fits

- **Cavos** fits consumer Starknet apps that want an audited standard account, sponsored gas, and the same SDK on Solana and Stellar.
- **Cartridge Controller** fits onchain games that need session keys and a gaming identity layer.
- **Chipi Pay** fits payments apps, especially in Latin America, that want passkey wallets plus bill payment services.
- **Privy** and **MetaMask Embedded Wallets** fit teams already on those platforms for EVM that are willing to build the Starknet account and gas layer themselves.

## Where Cavos stands on Starknet

Cavos is a non-custodial embedded wallet for Starknet, Solana and Stellar. On Starknet it deploys an audited OpenZeppelin account contract controlled by a stark key derived on the device, with Gas Sponsorship in Any Token through a paymaster.
