An [embedded wallet](/embedded-wallet) lives inside your application. **Embedded wallet cost** is the invoice for provisioning that wallet: per monthly active user, per wallet create, or per signature. This guide is how much embedded wallets cost in 2026 on published list prices for Privy, Dynamic, Turnkey, Para, Web3Auth / MetaMask Embedded Wallets, Alchemy, and Cavos. It is not [Cavos pricing](/pricing). That page is Cavos’s own fees. This page is the cross-vendor model, a worked example at 10,000 / 100,000 / 1,000,000 users using only public numbers, and a pricing-transparency checklist for enterprises.

Cavos, LLC is a software and SaaS company. Positioning for Cavos comes after the table is fair. If the honest published number is another vendor’s, that is the number. Where a public page omits a figure, or disagrees with itself, this page says so instead of guessing.

## The short answer (November 2026)

**How much embedded wallets cost** depends on the meter, not the logo.

- **Per-MAU / per-MAW:** Privy, Dynamic, Para, and Web3Auth / MetaMask Embedded Wallets. A user who logs in once in the month is billed; extra signatures may or may not add a second meter.
- **Per-signature:** Turnkey. Self-serve wallet caps are 1,000 (Pay as you go) and 2,000 (Pro). Unlimited wallets are Enterprise.
- **Per-wallet-create:** Cavos. Free for the first 1,000 creates, then a flat Essential $59/month or Complete $139/month. See [pricing](/pricing). Gas sponsorship is funded separately.
- **Not a wallet-create product:** Alchemy Wallet APIs bill compute units. Account Kit embedded wallets are deprecated.

**Privy wallets cost**, on the public Developer page: Free 0–499 MAU, Core $299/month, Scale $499/month. Past 10,000 MAU or 50,000 signatures, Privy publishes PAYG at a $2,000 base plus $0.05 per extra MAU plus $0.01 per extra signature. That is Privy wallet pricing as listed, not an Enterprise quote.

**What's the cheapest way to create wallets for millions of users?** On published self-serve list prices in this set, a flat create allowance (Cavos Essential at $59/month after 1,000 free creates) does not grow with MAU. MAU and signature meters do. Enterprise volume discounts are unpublished, so this page does not call any vendor the cheapest in the market.

A MAU on Privy is a Privy-authenticated user with at least one session in the last 30 days. A Dynamic MAU is a login or an embedded-wallet create in the month. A Para MAU is a user authenticated to or created in the application that month. A MetaMask Embedded Wallets MAW is a user who logs in or has an active session at least once in a calendar month. Those definitions are close, not identical. Do not mix them in a spreadsheet without a footnote.

Cavos counts **wallet creates**, not monthly actives. Existing wallets, reads, and signatures are not the create cap. When Free hits 1,000 creates, only new creates pause.

## What drives cost at millions of users

At a few hundred users, free tiers dominate and the vendors look similar. At millions, the meter is the product.

**MAU and MAW scale linearly** once you are off the included band. Privy’s public PAYG MAU line is $0.05 above 10,000. MetaMask Embedded Wallets’ public Scale overage is $0.040 above 10,000 MAWs. Para’s published extra-MAU falls from $0.06 to $0.04 as the plan steps up, then the FAQ hands 100k to sales.

**Signatures scale with activity, not headcount.** Turnkey’s public self-serve rates are $0.10 then $0.05 per signature. Privy adds a second meter at $0.01 per signature above 50,000 on PAYG. A quiet million-user app and a trading app are different bills on the same logo. This page does not invent a typical signatures-per-user number.

**Wallet caps become a sales conversation.** Turnkey Pay as you go lists 1,000 wallets; Pro lists 2,000; unlimited is Enterprise. You cannot stay on those self-serve wallet caps at 10,000 users.

**A create allowance does not grow with MAU.** Cavos Essential and Complete are flat after the first 1,000 creates. That is why the 10k / 100k / 1M cells do not change. It is also why this page must not be confused with [pricing](/pricing): that page is the Cavos fee table, not a market ranking.

**Enterprise is where most millions-of-users deals actually close**, and those rates are not on the public page. Privy, Dynamic, Turnkey, Para, Web3Auth, and Alchemy all say so. Modeling PAYG list prices as the production bill at 1M users is a starting bound, not a contract.

**Gas, RPC, and secrets sit next to the wallet fee.** Turnkey publishes gas-sponsorship caps on Pay as you go and Pro, and Managed Secrets at $0.15 per secret per month. Alchemy Gas Manager takes an 8% admin fee on PAYG. Cavos funds gas sponsorship separately from the org fee. Include those lines in a real model; do not hide them inside “wallet cost.”

## Privy wallets cost, in more detail

Searchers type **privy wallets cost** and **privy wallet pricing**. The public source is [privy.io/pricing](https://privy.io/pricing), read in November 2026.

Developer plans share unified login, embedded wallets, JWT-based authentication, and wallet connectors for EVM and Solana. All Developer plans include 50,000 signatures and $1M volume per month on the page’s “all plans include” copy; Free also states 0–499 MAU.

Exceeding a paid Developer MAU cap auto-upgrades to the next plan the following month. Exceeding 10,000 MAU or 50,000 signatures in a month moves usage to PAYG: $2,000 volume base, $0.05 per MAU above 10,000, $0.01 per signature above 50,000. Privy tells teams that anticipate sustained PAYG to talk to sales; Enterprise is described as typically cheaper and more predictable than monthly PAYG. That cheaper rate is not listed.

Canceling a subscription drops feature access and support beyond 500 MAUs; if the app has already reached 500 MAUs, new users can no longer sign up, per the public FAQ.

## Pricing-transparency checklist for enterprises

Counsel and procurement should get written answers. If a vendor cannot answer on a public page, ask for it in the MSA. Do not fill the blank from a blog post.

- **Meter.** Creates, MAU, MAW, signatures, compute units, transacting wallets, or volume. Who counts as active, and over what window.
- **Public list versus sales-gated.** Which numbers are on the pricing page, and which appear only after a call.
- **Overage.** Extra-MAU, extra-signature, extra-secret, compute-unit, and gas-admin lines. Whether two public sources on the same site disagree (Dynamic’s extra-MAU is the current example).
- **Included versus add-on.** Recovery, gas sponsorship, webhooks, SSO, pregenerated wallets, SLAs, dedicated support. Which of those flip at Enterprise.
- **Custody and export.** Who can produce a signature without the user. Whether key export is documented. Cavos restores a MasterDEK on a new device and does not advertise seed or private-key export. Privy user wallets document export. Dynamic documents export. Turnkey lists import and export keys.
- **SOC 2 and audits, as published.** MetaMask Embedded Wallets lists SOC 2 Type II on the product page. Para’s pricing FAQ lists SOC 2 Type II. Cavos does not publish a SOC 2 report on this site; the Starknet account is an **audited OpenZeppelin account contract**. The audit covers OpenZeppelin’s contract, not Cavos. Do not treat an account-contract audit as a vendor SOC 2.
- **SLAs.** Privy, Dynamic, Turnkey, and MetaMask Embedded Wallets list SLAs on Enterprise. Cavos does not publish an uptime SLA on [pricing](/pricing).
- **Lock-in.** What happens if you cancel (Privy publishes a 500-MAU cutoff for new sign-ups). Whether addresses migrate. They usually do not.

The [custody](/custody) page is the control test. The [wallet-as-a-service](/blog/wallet-as-a-service) guide is the platform map. This page is the invoice.

## Where Cavos fits

Cavos is a hardware-isolated, non-custodial [embedded wallet](/embedded-wallet), not an extension, and not a connector. Signing keys are created and used on the user’s device. Private keys never leave the device in plaintext. Signing always happens on the user’s device. The Nitro enclave and passkey only store an encrypted copy of the master key for restore. Cavos cannot see keys, sign with them, or move funds. There is no MPC and no hosted spend key.

On Starknet the on-chain account is an **audited OpenZeppelin account contract** (AccountUpgradeable v3.0.0). Cavos does not ship its own account implementation. Solana and Stellar are native adapters. Auth is hosted Google, Apple, or email, or an identity you pass in.

**Published Cavos fees**, short and only so this comparison has a Cavos row: Free for the first 1,000 wallet creates, Essential at $59/month, Complete at $139/month with enclave recovery. The full SDK ships on every plan. Gas sponsorship is funded separately. The fee table, recovery fork, and FAQ live on [pricing](/pricing). This post does not restate that page.

**Choose Cavos** when the chain is Starknet, Solana, or Stellar, the review requires that the vendor cannot reconstruct a signing key, and a published create-based fee is the commercial constraint.

**Choose someone else** when the product is EVM-first, when you must connect an existing extension, when you need server-side signing as a product feature, or when a sales-gated Enterprise pack (SLA, SSO, volume MAU) is the deal you are actually buying. Cavos does not inject into `window.ethereum` and does not speak WalletConnect.
