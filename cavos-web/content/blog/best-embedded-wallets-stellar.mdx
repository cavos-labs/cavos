This guide lists [embedded wallet](/embedded-wallet) SDKs that support **Stellar** today, based on each vendor's own documentation as of October 2026. Cavos, LLC publishes this page and Cavos is on the list, so the criteria come first and every row links to the source. If another provider fits your app better, use it.

For the general multichain view, see [embedded wallet providers](/blog/embedded-wallet-providers). For a hands-on build, see the [Stellar embedded wallet tutorial](/blog/stellar-embedded-wallet-tutorial).

## How we evaluated

- **Stellar depth.** Does the SDK create, sign, and submit Stellar transactions, or does it only derive a key and leave the rest to you?
- **Account model.** Classic G account or Soroban smart contract wallet.
- **Fees and reserves.** Can the app sponsor the base reserve and network fees so a new user does not need XLM first?
- **Custody.** Who can produce a signature, and what happens if the provider disappears.
- **Pricing meter.** Wallets, monthly active users, or signatures.

## When each one fits

- **Cavos** fits consumer Stellar apps that want a classic account, sponsored reserves and fees, and one SDK that also covers Solana and Starknet. It does not cover EVM.
- **Dynamic** fits teams that need Stellar next to EVM, Solana, Bitcoin, and others, plus external wallet connection, and can fund fees themselves on Stellar.
- **Crossmint** fits apps that want Soroban smart wallets with on-chain signer and recovery logic, often alongside its payments and onramp products.
- **Para** fits teams already on Para for EVM or Solana that want Stellar under the same MPC wallet.
- **Blux** fits Stellar-only dApps that want one login surface covering new users and existing Stellar wallets.
- **Privy** and **Turnkey** fit teams comfortable building and submitting Stellar transactions themselves on top of a signing layer.

## Where Cavos stands on Stellar

Cavos is a non-custodial embedded wallet for Starknet, Solana and Stellar. On Stellar it creates a classic G account whose Ed25519 spend key is derived on the device. A new device restores the key with another signed-in device, a passkey, or opt-in hardware-isolated enclave recovery. The Cavos relayer sponsors reserves and fee bumps, which is part of Gas Sponsorship in Any Token (for example, letting a user pay in USDC instead of XLM). Read the [Stellar wallet page](/embedded-stellar-wallet), the [custody model](/custody), [pricing](/pricing), and the [quickstart](https://docs.cavos.xyz/docs/quickstart).
